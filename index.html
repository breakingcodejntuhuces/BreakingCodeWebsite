Who We Are

About Breaking Code

Breaking Code is the official coding club of JNTUH University College of Engineering Sultanpur (UCES), established on 2 March 2026 with a vision to cultivate a strong coding culture across the campus. Founded through the collaboration of the Computer Science & Engineering (CSE), CSE (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning), and CSE (Cyber Security) 2024-2028 students, the club empowers students to learn by building, solving, and competing. We believe that the best way to master programming is through consistent practice. From coding contests and problem-solving sessions to workshops and technical events, Breaking Code provides a platform where students sharpen their logical thinking, strengthen algorithmic skills, and grow together as a community. Whether you're writing your first program or preparing for competitive programming and placements, Breaking Code offers an environment where curiosity is encouraged, challenges become opportunities, and every member has the chance to improve.
